# Process presets: ship two or three ready-made processes

## Description

Process presets: ship two or three ready-made processes

Teams judge SDD tools on whether they match how they already plan work (Thoughtworks Radar still rates SDD 'Assess'). Process-as-config makes the process declarable but every team starts from the same default overlay. Ship named presets on top of doc-types.yaml — e.g. 'rfc-heavy' (spec review required, ADRs, retro required), 'lightweight-adr' (ideas + decisions, review optional), 'issue-driven' (GitHub issues as the unit, mattpocock-adapter style) — selectable at 'rcorn init' and switchable later. Analogous to Spec Kit presets/bundles (33 presets, role bundles). Source: landscape research 2026-10-02.
