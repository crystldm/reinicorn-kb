# Scale-adaptive process depth: gates sized to the change

## Description

Scale-adaptive process depth: gates sized to the change

The loudest criticism of SDD (Böckeler on martinfowler.com, 'SDD keeps ending up as waterfall') is ceremony on small changes. BMAD v6 answers with scale-adaptive tracks (Quick Flow / Method / Enterprise) picked per change. Reinicorn could let process-as-config gates condition on change size/kind (files or lines touched, paths, labels): a typo fix needs no spec or retro; a cross-cutting change requires spec review + retro. Builds directly on the existing 'policy-language-for-overlay-conditions' idea. Source: landscape research 2026-10-02.
